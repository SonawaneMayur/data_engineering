Traditional ETL Flow Databricks
E
Source Systems

▼
T

Architectural Note: Data Lakehouse natively discourages this because it discards the original raw files, preventing you from reprocessing or re-extracting later if business rules change.
The ELT Paradigm (Load Instantly / Transform Inside)
Modern Native ELT Pipeline Snowflake
E L
Snowpipe / COPY INTO
Extracts files and dumps raw variant JSON/CSV immediately into a Stage
▼
L
Snowflake Internal Stage / Landing Table
Raw structural state. Low cost storage. Unparsed.
▼
T
Virtual Warehouse Compute Layer
Executes internal SQL, dbt pipelines, or Snowpark (Python) scripts
▼
L
Final Production Tables
Loads fully modeled columns into structured, optimized micro-partitions
Architectural Note: Highly recommended for Snowflake. Keeps a lightweight ingestion pipeline and shifts all data processing costs strictly into elastic Virtual Warehouse compute.
